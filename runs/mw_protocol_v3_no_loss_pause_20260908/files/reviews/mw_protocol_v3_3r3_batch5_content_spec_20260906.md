## Assessments

Define endpoint measurement methods, named instruments and versions, evaluator
roles/training, specimen type/volume/handling, relevant timing and qualifications.
Do not substitute a generic “per SOA” paragraph for how a measurement is obtained.
Conversely, bind timing to the actual SOA rather than create conflicting duplicates.
Eligibility assessments, efficacy assessments and safety monitoring have distinct
purposes even when they share a procedure.

Body 574–575 carries specimens and generic special assays: neither constitutes a
complete immunogenicity strategy. Applicable PK/PD/E-R and immunogenicity need
their own linked endpoints, samples, timing and analysis requirements. Future-use
specimens are distinct from samples required by the current study.

IRC (586–589) is conditional. RECIST 1.1, oncology imaging and CD transfer are
examples, not universal endpoint adjudication methods. Do not conflate an IRC
with a safety/data monitoring committee. Parent content and these unheaded
obligations remain covered despite leaf counting.

## Event content and template issues

- AE definition, collection interval, TEAE derivation, severity, seriousness,
  causality, expectedness, recording and follow-up are distinct obligations.
- Source 599 oncology progression exception cannot be copied to unrelated
  indications. Source 602 CTCAE 5.0 is a versioned selection requiring applicable
  confirmation, not evidence that it is always the latest or correct scale.
- Preserve user-approved five-category causality terminology. Source 634 rapid-
  reporting treatment of “可能无关” and source 639 binary mapping need separate
  disposition; a statistical ADR grouping must not silently become the expedited
  reporting algorithm. Draft-guidance quotation at 635 is not current final law.
- Source 652 mixes consent-to-follow-up collection with pre-dose history-only
  treatment. Define the actual collection periods and handling explicitly; do
  not silently equate AE collection with TEAE classification. Thirty days,
  five half-lives and new anticancer treatment are not universal cutoffs.
- Source 679 allows choosing report time for convenience. Preserve occurrence,
  sampling/assessment, awareness and reporting times separately where relevant;
  do not replace a clinical date simply because tracing it is harder.
- AE terminology and seriousness criteria are separate from treatments/outcomes.
  Follow-up and the clinical handling of ongoing events need actual scope and
  duration; nonempty event labels alone cannot satisfy this contract.
- Source 713 investigator reporting, 723–731 sponsor expedited reporting and
  ethics/institution communications need distinct recipient/timing obligations.
  Source 729 “signed valid report” as day zero must not delay first-awareness
  reporting; previously reviewed ICH E2A III.B is an anchor, while jurisdiction-
  specific implementation still requires the applicable current authority.
- AESI definitions and thresholds (739–747) need product-specific selection;
  aPTT/INR grade 3 and QTc grade 2 are examples, not default AESIs.
- Pregnancy testing, prevention, exposure reporting, partner consent, outcomes
  and abnormal-outcome event reporting are distinct. Source 749's three/six-month
  periods are examples, not a universal rule. Source 750 must not force complete
  study withdrawal merely because treatment is stopped for pregnancy. Follow-up
  and further-data collection require appropriate project-specific arrangements.

## Fixture distinctions

Wrong scale/version; missing specimen volume or assay purpose; comparator evidence
used for own-product safety claim; empty monitoring schedule; SAE classified from
severity alone; copied oncology exceptions; signed-form requirement delaying a
report; ADR mapping reused blindly for expedited reporting; pregnancy equated
with SAE or automatic total withdrawal; invented medical or approval receipts.

Deterministic fixtures can verify declared values, links and contradictions.
They cannot establish medical adequacy, current law or committee approval solely
from a submitted `passed` flag or a document filename.
