# 3R3 batch 7: data and ethics content preparation

Preparation only. Codex reread source body children 808–881 directly, approved
TP-MA-07 v2.0 unchanged. These are clinical protocol content obligations, not an
authorization to build excluded security features in the workbench.

- Data capture, source identification, review/query resolution, corrections,
  lock/export and responsibilities require actual study arrangements. EDC is a
  source example, not proof the project has an EDC vendor or validated system.
- Computerized-system and data-governance statements must reflect actual roles,
  records and applicable arrangements, not fabricated completed validation.
  Direct eCRF source data and copied source records need distinct identification.
- Future sample/data use (829–836) is separate from required current-study assays:
  purpose, location, duration, genetic testing, relevant consent and future-use
  review must be explicit when applicable. Source 835's post-study inability to
  withdraw consent is not a universal conclusion; project/legal review required.
- Record retention (837–842) needs applicable trigger, duration, custody and
  disposition arrangements. Do not copy five years, optical discs, or “all records
  owned by sponsor” as universally correct law or infrastructure.
- Publication/data sharing (843–849) must reflect actual agreements and applicable
  requirements; do not fabricate a repository, committee or publication permission.
- Ethics and consent (850–856) require appropriate prospective procedure, versions,
  responsible roles, timing before relevant study procedures, applicable capacity/
  representative/witness arrangements and new-information handling. A witness is
  not a universal substitute for participant consent. Avoid implying approval
  already obtained merely because this is an approval-ready protocol.
- Confidentiality (862–879) must specify applicable data/sample coding, recipients,
  access and transfer arrangements without inventing actual people or facilities.
- Compensation, trial-related injury arrangements and insurance/guarantee
  (880–881) are required content with genuine project facts or referenced actual
  agreements. Missing facts must trigger intake/recommendation resolution, not
  make the section inapplicable or create fabricated amounts/coverage.

The source's specific GCP article numbers and retention/consent legal wording are
not independently validated by this XML read. Keep them as provenance pending
applicable-primary-source review. This is not a claim of clinical/legal release.

Negative fixtures include nonexistent repositories, blank custody/period fields,
copied company names, unsigned plans claimed approved, study data conflated with
optional future use, missing mandatory compensation arrangement, and future-use
permission inferred from ordinary trial consent. Structural lints cannot establish
ethics approval or legal adequacy from the presence of a document reference.
